GREEK SALAD

Description

This Greek salad recipe is perfect for summer when tomatoes and cucumbers taste best. The dressing is nice and tangy, and when mixed with feta — this salad tastes incredible!

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 (6 ounce) can pitted black olives
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 6 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Combine cucumber, tomatoes, olives, bell peppers, and red onion in a large bowl; sprinkle with feta cheese.
  2. Combine romaine, cucumber, tomatoes, olives, bell peppers, and red onion in a large bowl; sprinkle with feta cheese.
  3. Pour dressing over salad, toss well to combine, and serve.
